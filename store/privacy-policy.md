# Milvaflow privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Milvaflow is made by MilvaLabs. It helps you start MTN Mobile Money payments
by dialing the right USSD code for you. This policy explains what the app
does with your information. In short: everything stays on your phone.

## What Milvaflow does not do

- It has no internet access. It cannot send anything anywhere.
- It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- It never sees your Mobile Money PIN. You type your PIN on MTN's own
  screen, which Milvaflow cannot read.
- It cannot see your balance or whether a payment succeeded.

## Contacts

If you allow it, Milvaflow reads the names and phone numbers in your
contacts so you can search them when choosing who to pay. Contacts are read
on your phone each time you open the list. They are not copied, stored or
shared. You can use Milvaflow without allowing contacts by typing the number
yourself, and you can turn the permission off at any time in your phone's
settings.

## Phone calls

Milvaflow asks for permission to make phone calls. It uses it only to dial
the MTN USSD code for a payment you chose, for example
`*182*1*1*<number>*<amount>#`. It does not make or read any other calls.

## Recent payments

To let you repeat a payment in one tap, Milvaflow keeps a list of up to 8
recent payments on your phone: the recipient's name (if it came from your
contacts), their number, MoMo Pay code or bank account number, the amount,
and the time. This list never leaves your phone. You can remove an entry by
pressing and holding it, and uninstalling the app deletes the whole list.

## Children

Milvaflow is meant for adults who use Mobile Money. It is not directed at
children.
